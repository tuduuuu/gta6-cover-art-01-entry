Sunset Strip Cover Maker turns your own photos and a caption into multi-panel GTA-style poster cover art — three layouts, four colour grades, drag-to-position photos — and exports it as a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080.

## Features

- Upload up to 3 of your own photos, or use the two built-in sample scenes (painted in code, no borrowed images).
- Three multi-panel layouts: Poster Grid, Triple Strip, and Hero.
- Four colour grades: Sunset, Neon Nights, Dusk, and Noir.
- Click a panel to select it, drag to reposition its photo, zoom with the slider, double-click to reset.
- Editable title and subtitle with gradient fill, heavy outline, and glow.
- Export as PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350, or 1920×1080. Works fully offline — no network requests, no uploads.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser. See the entry and its cover art on [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).
